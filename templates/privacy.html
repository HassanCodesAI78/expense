Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide when you create an account and use Spendly: your email address, your password, and the expense records you enter — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

Spendly does not connect to your bank or any financial institution, and we do not collect account numbers, card details, or transaction data from third parties. Everything we hold about your spending is what you have entered yourself.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to operate the service for you: to authenticate your account, display your expenses and category breakdowns, and produce the summaries and date-range views you request.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising or profiling.

3. Data Storage

Your account details and expense records are stored on the servers that host Spendly for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable measures to protect this data, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You can request deletion of your account and the data associated with it at any time by contacting us using the details below. We will action such requests within a reasonable period.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share your data with third-party analytics, advertising, or data-broker services. The service is provided using hosting infrastructure only, and your data is not disclosed to third parties except where required by law.

If we introduce third-party services that process your data in future, we will update this policy and revise the "last updated" date above before those services take effect.

5. Contact Us

Questions about this policy, or requests to access or delete your data? Contact us at support@spendly.example.
